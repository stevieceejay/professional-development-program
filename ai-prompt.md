# AI Prompt Literacy Guide

AI is a partner in your growth. Strong prompts lead to better outcomes. Use this guide to practice clarity, context, iteration, and reflection.

## Core Prompt Principles
1. Clarity — Define the goal (rewrite, compare, generate plan).  
2. Context — Provide background (role, level, target organization).  
3. Constraints — Set limits (word count, tone, format).  
4. Iteration — Ask for improvements (version 2, add metrics, simplify).  
5. Reflection — Evaluate output: what changes help you grow?

## Starter Prompts (Copy, then personalize)
- Compare MBTI types:  
  “I’m ENFP. Compare ENFP vs ISTJ teamwork styles. Give 3 collaboration tips for cross-type teams.”

- Resume summary refinement:  
  “Rewrite my resume summary to emphasize leadership and adaptability for entry-level IT roles. 60–80 words, professional tone.”

- 90-day job search plan:  
  “Generate a 90-day plan for entry-level IT support roles in Los Angeles: weekly application targets, networking actions, and skill-building steps.”

- Outreach email:  
  “Draft a professional email to a nonprofit recruiter introducing me, referencing their mission, and requesting an informational interview. 150 words.”

- Feedback integration:  
  “Review this cover letter paragraph for clarity and tone. Suggest 3 edits that better align with [Pathway To Kindship]’s mission.”

- Video reflection outline:  
  “Create a 6-bullet outline for a 2-minute video reflecting on my growth, goals, and how my skills support an organization’s mission.”

## Ethical & Professional Use
- Respect privacy, avoid sharing sensitive data.  
- Use AI as an assistant, not a replacement for your judgment.  
- Tailor outputs to each organization (dynamic resume + cover letter).  
- Credit collaboration when appropriate (“AI-assisted drafts; final edits by me”).

## Outcome
By Week 12, you will be **AI fluent**: capable of prompting for resumes, cover letters, strategies, outreach, and reflection — and integrating feedback to produce professional, mission-aligned work.
