# Privacy Policy

We collect and process the following data:
- Account information: username, email, role.
- Activity: lesson views, assignment submissions, and visit logs (path, method, user agent, IP, timestamp).
- Uploaded files: stored under `/uploads/<userId>/...` for program recordkeeping.

Purpose of processing:
- Provide curriculum access, track participation, and enable admin review.
- Generate reports for nonprofit partners to demonstrate impact.

Data sharing:
- Submissions may be shared with external partners or employers upon Admin request. Sharing is limited to assignment content and relevant metadata. Email sharing requires explicit recipient addresses.

Retention:
- Visit logs and submissions are retained for reporting and historical records. Admins can request deletion of specific user records in accordance with organizational policy.

Security:
- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt.
- Sessions are secured server-side; configure HTTPS in production.
- Access to admin functions requires the Admin role.

Your rights:
- Users may request a copy of their submissions and visit logs.
- Users may request correction or deletion where applicable.
