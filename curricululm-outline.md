# 12-Week Curriculum Outline (Rolling Enrollment)

Program design: self-paced modules, weekly Zoom (one session for all active learners), nonprofit proof of service at each step, spiritual philosophy integrated throughout.

---

## Week 1 — Professional Mindset & Spiritual Connection
**Purpose:** Establish self-awareness and professional attitudes rooted in a spiritual view of work.  
**Learning Goals:**  
- MBTI discovery; reflect on teamwork, feedback, and problem-solving  
- Understand work as a spiritual connection: mutual growth for person and organization  
**Deliverables:**  
- MBTI result  
- 300–500 word essay on personality fit, teamwork, feedback  
- Commitment statement aligning personal growth with organizational goals  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Submissions timestamped and visible in dashboard.

---

## Week 2 — Digital Education I: Gmail & Drive
**Purpose:** Build professional communication and file management.  
**Learning Goals:** Email etiquette, signatures, filters; Drive folder setup and secure sharing  
**Deliverables:**  
- Professional email draft  
- Program folder in Drive with organized docs  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Email draft + folder link logged.

---

## Week 3 — Digital Education II: Docs, Sheets, Slides
**Purpose:** Create artifacts that immediately support resume building.  
**Learning Goals:**  
- Resume draft in Docs  
- Job application tracker in Sheets  
- Short presentation in Slides  
**Deliverables:** Resume draft, tracker, presentation file  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Files stored and timestamped.

---

## Week 4 — Digital Education III: Notion, Zoom & Slack
**Purpose:** Time management and collaboration readiness.  
**Learning Goals:** Notion dashboard for scheduling; Zoom/Slack participation etiquette  
**Deliverables:** Notion dashboard screenshot; Zoom/Slack participation proof  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Dashboard entries with artifacts.

---

## Week 5 — Resume Fundamentals
**Purpose:** Learn resume structure, formatting, and professional language.  
**Deliverables:** Draft resume (core sections complete)  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Submission logged.

---

## Week 6 — Resume Refinement
**Purpose:** Integrate peer/AI feedback; improve clarity and impact.  
**Deliverables:** Revised resume (tracked changes or version history)  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Versioned file and timestamp.

---

## Week 7 — Cover Letter Fundamentals
**Purpose:** Persuasive writing tailored to role and organization.  
**Deliverables:** Draft cover letter linked to a target job description  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Submission logged.

---

## Week 8 — Cover Letter Refinement
**Purpose:** Integrate feedback and align tone with mission and values.  
**Deliverables:** Final cover letter  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Version history recorded.

---

## Week 9 — Job Search Strategy (90-Day Plan)
**Purpose:** Structured actions across applications, networking, and skill growth.  
**Deliverables:** Written 90-day plan with weekly milestones  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Plan stored in dashboard.

---

## Week 10 — Networking & Communication Skills
**Purpose:** Outreach, LinkedIn presence, interview etiquette.  
**Deliverables:** Sample outreach email/message + brief reflection  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Submission logged.

---

## Week 11 — Video Reflection Assignment
**Purpose:** Articulate growth, goals, and alignment with organizational missions.  
**Deliverables:** 1–3 minute video file  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Timestamped upload.

---

## Week 12 — Final Portfolio & Completion
**Purpose:** Compile a dynamic, adaptable portfolio.  
**Deliverables:**  
- Final resume  
- Final cover letter  
- 90-day plan  
- Personality survey + reflection  
- Video reflection  
**Nonprofit Proof:** Portfolio submission; dashboard marks completion; optional completion report.

---

## Weekly Zoom — Flexibility Model
- One open session per week for Q&A, reflection, and accountability  
- Learners at any stage can attend (rolling enrollment)  
- Zoom agenda highlights current week’s theme; newcomers get orientation; advanced learners get targeted support

---

## Philosophy Integration Notes
- “Business is spiritual” embedded in Week 1 and revisited during resume/cover letter tailoring  
- Resumes and cover letters are **dynamic**: updated for each role to honor the unique connection with the organization  
- AI is a partner: use prompts for insight, iteration, and refinement across modules
